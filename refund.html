    • Refund Policy

    Learn about our refund and cancellation policy for EduLearn courses.

    1. Course Registration

    Students can register for courses available on the EduLearn platform according to their interests.

    2. Refund Eligibility

    A refund may be requested if a student has not started the course or has a valid reason for cancellation.

    3. Refund Request

    Students can contact the EduLearn support team to request a refund. The request should include the student's name, email address and course name.

    4. Refund Processing

    After reviewing the request, eligible refunds will be processed within a reasonable period.

    5. Non-Refundable Cases

    Refunds may not be available after significant course content has been accessed or completed.

    6. Course Cancellation

    If EduLearn cancels a course, registered students may be eligible for a refund according to the circumstances.

    7. Contact Us

    If you have any questions about our Refund Policy, please contact us through the Contact Us page.
